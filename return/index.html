The stops

Tap "We're here" at each stop, or in the bar at the bottom, and the car moves along the road. Not stopping somewhere? Tap Skip and every time below adjusts. Leave a note or a photo tag under Memories at each stop. Pumps appear between the stops, and the Fuel stops switch above hides them.

    Road heads-up

    Drivers on a Bengaluru to Pondicherry forum describe the Krishnagiri to Uthangarai stretch as rough and patched. Going home you meet it in the afternoon at the default 9 AM start, between Uthangarai and Krishnagiri.

    • Aim to clear that stretch in daylight. The Race the sunset bar above tells you if your plan does.
    • Hosur Road and the approach to Mahadevapura can crawl on weekend and holiday evenings. The traffic buffer switch adds 45 minutes so the arrival time is honest.
    • If the rough stretch looks bad, the Vellore, Arcot and Cheyyar road is the easier option, about 40 km longer.

    Sources: Bangalore to Pondicherry forum thread and route comparison guide. Forum reports can be old, so trust what you see on the road.

      Pumps to skip
      HPCL, Uranitangal (Gingee)

      Several reviews report mixed petrol. Use the IndianOil pump on Villupuram Main Road instead.

      BPCL Sri Balaji, Tindivanam

      One review describes the wrong nozzle nearly going into a petrol car. Use the Salavadi pump at the Aishwarya junction, a few minutes further on.

      IndianOil, Kanji Road, Tiruvannamalai

      Multiple complaints about billing and short fills. Use the NH 66 or Polur Road pumps.
