Tap "We're here" at each stop, or in the bar at the bottom, and the car moves along the road. Not stopping somewhere? Tap Skip and every time below adjusts. Leave a note or a photo tag under Memories at each stop. Pumps appear between the stops, and the Fuel stops switch above hides them.

    Road heads-up

    The expressway bypasses Bidadi, Ramanagara, Channapatna, Maddur, Mandya and Srirangapatna, so it is quick, but the first 38 km out of Mahadevapura to Kengeri are the slow part at 7 AM.

    • Expect tolls both ways and keep the FASTag topped up. Speed limits on the expressway are enforced, so stay under the posted limit.
    • The drive back lands you in Bengaluru in the evening rush. The traffic buffer switch adds 30 minutes so the arrival time is honest.
    • The Race the sunset bar shows how much of the way home is in daylight. Sunset is around 5:45 PM at this time of year.

    Sources: Outlook Traveller road trip guide and the Team-BHP expressway thread. Forum reports can be old, so trust what you see on the road.

      About Shell on this road
      Shell is thin on this corridor

      I found Shell outlets at Kengeri (Mysore Road), Ramanagara (B.M. Road) and Hinkal on Hunsur Road in Mysuru. I found none confirmed at Channapatna, Maddur or Mandya, so IndianOil covers Mandya.

      Check before you rely on a pin

      Shell opening hours and amenities were not confirmed for the three Shell sites, and I only have addresses, not exact pins. Open each in Maps and check it is open.

      One tank is enough

      A tank filled at Kengeri covers most of the round trip. A top-up at Ramanagara or Mandya on the way back leaves a safe reserve.
