The stops

Tap "We're here" at each stop, or in the bar at the bottom, and the car moves along the road. Not stopping somewhere? Tap Skip and every time below adjusts. Pumps appear between the stops, and the Fuel stops switch above hides them.

    Road heads-up

    Drivers on a Bengaluru to Pondicherry forum describe the Krishnagiri to Uthangarai stretch as rough and patched, and call the route through Vellore, Arcot and Cheyyar the easier one. That route runs about 350 km instead of 310.

    • This plan takes the direct road through Tiruvannamalai and Gingee.
    • If the rough stretch looks bad in daylight, switch to the Vellore route. Shoolagiri and Tindivanam work as stops on both.
    • Do the Krishnagiri to Uthangarai stretch while there is plenty of light.

    Sources: Bangalore to Pondicherry forum thread and route comparison guide. Forum reports can be old, so trust what you see on the road.

      Pumps to skip
      HPCL, Uranitangal (Gingee)

      Several reviews report mixed petrol. Use the IndianOil pump on Villupuram Main Road instead.

      BPCL Sri Balaji, Tindivanam

      One review describes the wrong nozzle nearly going into a petrol car. Use the Salavadi pump at the Aishwarya junction.

      IndianOil, Kanji Road, Tiruvannamalai

      Multiple complaints about billing and short fills. Use the NH 66 or Polur Road pumps.
